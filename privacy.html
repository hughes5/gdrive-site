Privacy Policy

This site supports the gdrive-backup rclone remote used to store restic backup archives on Google Drive.

Data we collect

None. This site does not collect, store, or process any personal data.

Backup data

Backup archives uploaded to Google Drive are encrypted with restic before leaving the backup host. Google stores the encrypted bytes; we cannot read them.
